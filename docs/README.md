# OctoAcme Project Management Docs

A guide to OctoAcme's project management processes and practices. OctoAcme manages cross-functional work through a lightweight lifecycle: initiate work by validating the business need, stakeholders, and success measures; plan by defining scope, a prioritized backlog, ownership, milestones, risks, and dependencies; execute iteratively with clear acceptance criteria, regular coordination, testing, and progress tracking; release with readiness checks, deployment verification, and rollback planning; then capture learning through retrospectives and continuous improvement.

Project Managers coordinate delivery, schedules, risks, and communications. Product Managers define outcomes, prioritize the backlog, and measure success. Developers implement and test features, QA validates acceptance, and stakeholders provide input and approvals. Teams track work on a project board, use small pull requests linked to issues and acceptance criteria, and escalate blockers through the agreed path.

Communication includes delivery-team standups, regular PM and Product Manager alignment, weekly delivery or stakeholder updates, and demos at sprint or milestone ends. Risks and dependencies are recorded with owners and mitigation plans, reviewed regularly, and communicated through a shared source of truth. Quality assurance combines unit and integration testing where appropriate, end-to-end smoke tests for critical flows, security scanning in CI, and manual QA when needed. Releases require acceptance criteria and CI/security checks to pass, with release notes, smoke tests, and rollback or mitigation plans prepared. Retrospectives turn lessons into owned, time-bound improvement actions.
