# OctoAcme Project Management Docs

A guide to OctoAcme's project management processes and practices. OctoAcme manages cross-functional work through a lightweight lifecycle: initiate work by validating the business need, stakeholders, and success measures; plan by defining scope, a prioritized backlog, ownership, milestones, risks, and dependencies; execute iteratively with clear acceptance criteria, regular coordination, testing, and progress tracking; release with readiness checks, deployment verification, and rollback planning; then capture learning through retrospectives and continuous improvement. Project Managers coordinate delivery, risks, schedules, and communications; Product Managers define outcomes and priorities; developers, QA, and stakeholders contribute to delivery and validation.
